Understanding Your Knee Data

Thigh Angle

The thigh angle represents the inclination of your upper leg relative to your body. Monitoring this angle helps ensure proper posture during rehabilitation exercises, preventing unnecessary strain.

Shin Angle

The shin angle indicates the alignment of your lower leg with the ground. It helps track the effectiveness of balance and stability during movement.

Knee Angle

The knee angle measures the bending or straightening of your knee joint. It is crucial for assessing the range of motion, which is vital for recovery progress and rehabilitation outcomes.

Gait Phase

The gait phase identifies the current stage of your walking cycle (e.g., stance or swing). This helps monitor walking patterns and detect improvements or irregularities during rehabilitation.
